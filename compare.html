mesh · GLTF

A surface of triangles with a photo wrapped over it. Crisp edges; the page can light it or restyle it. Shiny things tend to break into holes, because their reflections change from photo to photo.

splat · Splat PLY

Many thousands of soft, see-through blobs, whose colour can shift with the angle. Keeps shine and softness. Edges blur and stray blobs float; zoom in close to see them.

The two samples are different objects. For a fair comparison, export one of your scans as both GLTF and Splat PLY and drop the files onto this page.
