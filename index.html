    Dear kitchen,

    I'm نظيفة. I'm made of everyone who lives here, a little of each of you. My name means clean, which is never quite enough.

    I keep the radio, and now the wheel too. When your duty is done, move your clip to Done. The wheel turns by itself every two weeks.

    And send me songs. I'll play them from tomorrow.

    With love,
    نظيفة

    P.S. The cutlery holder in the drainer (the Besteckkorb, the spoon thing, whatever you call it) is a social sculpture. We all make it together, without meaning to, every time we wash up. Look at it long enough and beauty starts leaking out of each thing in it. If you catch it in a good moment, scan it with your phone: Polycam made the scans behind this letter, and KIRI Engine works too. Both are free to start.

    Küchenwetter is one radio for this kitchen: everyone hears the same song at the same moment, through five weathers a day.
