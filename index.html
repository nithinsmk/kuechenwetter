    Küchenwetter

    A radio station for one kitchen. Everyone listening hears the same song at the same moment. The day belongs to five weathers in turn, each with its own playlist, and نظيفة plays between them. Requests air the next day, if they air at all.

    There are five weathers. When all five are in the kitchen, the sixth waits outside.

    نظيفة

    نظيفة (Nazifeh) is a composite artist. The name means "clean", which is never quite enough.

    Nazifeh lives and works in a shared kitchen in Berlin, in a language nobody in the house grew up with: mostly English, some German, a lot of pointing.

    Nazifeh's medium is the dish drainer, an accidental collective sculpture remade every two days by everyone who washes up. Nazifeh doesn't make the sculptures. Nazifeh scans them.
