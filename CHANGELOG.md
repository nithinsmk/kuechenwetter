# Versions

Each working prototype is a git tag. Newest first.

| Version | Date | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v0.13 | 2026-10-09 | Little 8-bit plants for Salvia (`plants.js`): the mini acacia palm, the Japanese maple bonsai in its rectangular pot, a parlour palm and a fan palm, voxel-built and planted on the viewer's side of the counter (and on the hill). She eats them on her rounds; tap one and she runs to eat that one, bite by bite; leaves grow back after 90 seconds. |
| v0.12 | 2026-10-09 | Calling Salvia: a treat packet by the dates. Shake it (a crinkle you can hear, made in the browser, with little "krsh" words) and she comes running from wherever she is to a spot in view, turns to you, sits, rubs, tilts her head and purrs (also synthesised) for about 12 seconds, hearts over her head; more shaking keeps her longer. She's 2.5 times her first size. |
| v0.11 | 2026-10-09 | The site speaks to the flat. **The duty wheel**: a cardboard replica of the fridge's cleaning rotation in the corner (tap to open it big, drag your clip to Done; it turns by itself every two weeks from 3 Oct; moves go in a log behind a cartoon log button; `supabase/03-wheel.sql`). **The letter**: the about box is now نظيفة's very short letter to the kitchen, with a P.S. on the cutlery holder as a social sculpture and how to scan it, and one line on the radio; scan titles sit above the dates. **The gallery** (`gallery.html`): things cut out of the 28.09 scan (`tools/cut_character.py`) floating in a slow ring; the "gallery of 3D scans" button has little turning versions of them (`tools/sprites.html`) that chase the mouse with XP-style trails. **Salvia** (`salvia.js`), the house cat, lives in the scan at an eighth of the holder: arrives, checks in, sniffs, eats the plant, sleeps, runs off; tap her to change form (splat, pixel, cartoon). **The 8-bit hill** (`hill.js`): a blocky landscape after the desktop hill, for one random hour a day, or when someone pets Salvia ten times in a row. |
| v0.10 | 2026-10-09 | Every song streams as a 128 kbps MP3, to stretch the free streaming allowance (about 90 hours of listening a month instead of about 45). `keeper.py` and the desk convert whatever they're given (MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG, AIFF) with ffmpeg, without cover art or tags; an MP3 already at 128 kbps or below isn't encoded again. The 13 songs on air were swapped for 128 kbps versions with unchanged lengths. The desk shows the allowance as hours of listening. |
| v0.9.1 | 2026-10-09 | Fix: the live radio lost its scans and songs because the Pages exclude `radio` (meant for the keeper folder) also hid `radio.js` and `radio.css`. Excludes now match folder contents only (`radio/*`). |
| v0.9 | 2026-10-09 | Weather playlists. The Berlin day belongs to the five weathers in turn (about 4 h 48 min each), each playing its own playlist; at every change of weather one song by نظيفة plays first. Requests can suggest a weather. The keeper desk (`python3 desk/server.py`, http://localhost:8772/desk/): on air and next, the day strip, who's listening, storage, requests (drop a file on one to fulfil it), drag-and-drop loading and the playlists, with songs moved between weathers. Broadcast logic shared in `broadcast.js`. |
| v0.8 | 2026-10-08 | Ready for the mixtape: `keeper.py add-mix FOLDER --title T` adds a folder of songs as one show by Nazif Limpio Saaf that plays in order, each song named on air. The label now suits the fridge ("the sculpture is drying in the rack"). `?scan=2026-09-25` opens a date and `?clean` hides the interface, for documentation images. |
| v0.7 | 2026-10-04 | Songs can go on air the same day without breaking sync: `keeper.py today ID` (or `add … --today`) pings every open radio, which reloads the song list at once. |
| v0.6 | 2026-10-04 | Published at https://nithinsmk.github.io/kuechenwetter/ (repo nithinsmk/kuechenwetter, commits under GitHub's private email). Adds `label.html`, a printable QR label ("work in progress") pointing to it. |
